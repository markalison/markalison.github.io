How these odds work

  • Every remaining regular-season game is played out 20,000 times. Each game is decided by the rating gap plus 1.5 points for the home team, with 13 points of game randomness, and each team’s strength also drifts by 1.5 points per simulated season.
  • Ratings are grouped by how they calculate. Point-based: ESPN’s FPI, Sagarin, TeamRankings and this site’s points rating (this season’s scores, margins capped at 24, adjusted for opponent and home field). Elo: nfelo and a classic Elo run over every game since 1999 (25 Elo points to a point). Betting market: ratings fitted to this season’s point spreads from nflverse, recent weeks counting most. Recent form: TeamRankings’ last five games and Sagarin’s recent rating. The default averages one rating from each source: ESPN FPI, Sagarin Rating, TeamRankings Predictive, nfelo, Classic Elo, Point spreads, Points rating (this site).
  • Division titles and wild cards follow the NFL’s tiebreakers: head-to-head, division record, common games, conference record, strength of victory and strength of schedule, then a coin toss.
  • Seven teams per conference make the playoffs. The four division winners are seeded 1-4 and the three wild cards 5-7. Only the 1 seed gets a bye, and the top remaining seed hosts each game. The Super Bowl is on a neutral field.
  • Playoffs after a win or loss show the chance to make the playoffs if the team wins or loses its next game.
